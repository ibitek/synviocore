Legal / Privacy / Pilot

Clear boundaries before production.

This page describes the current private-pilot framework. A signed commercial agreement, order form or data-processing agreement can add to or replace these website terms for a specific customer.

Important

Before accepting normal production customers, add the operating entity’s complete legal identity, address and required company/tax identifiers and have customer-facing privacy/commercial terms reviewed for the applicable jurisdiction.

1. Product status and website information

Synvio is an evolving customer-support SaaS in private pilot. Screenshots, demo tickets, AI-chat simulations, suggested drafts and usage calculators on the marketing site may use illustrative data. They are not performance guarantees or representations that every displayed feature is production-live.

2. Private pilot

Pilot scope, connected inboxes, AI features, included usage, introductory pricing and operational responsibilities are agreed before activation. Features may change during pilot testing. Unless a signed agreement states otherwise, pilot access does not include a guaranteed uptime, response-time SLA or promise that a specific experimental feature will remain unchanged.

3. Accounts, organizations and multi-tenancy

Users must protect their credentials and access only organizations they are authorized to use. Synvio is designed as a multi-tenant service: organization-scoped records and database access controls are used to separate customer workspaces. Customers are responsible for inviting the correct employees and removing access when no longer required.

4. Mailboxes, sender identities and DNS

A customer may connect or authorize only mailboxes, domains and sender identities it controls or is legally authorized to use. Custom-domain onboarding may require DNS records or mailbox authorization. Customers should review DNS changes so they do not unintentionally disrupt existing mail services.

5. Web chat and human handoff

The product roadmap includes a customer-facing AI chat channel that can hand a conversation to a human operator inside the same Synvio workspace. Until the feature is explicitly enabled for a customer, marketing demonstrations of this workflow are illustrative. Customers remain responsible for disclosures and consent requirements applicable to their website chat deployment.

6. AI-assisted output

AI classifications, summaries, translations, retrieval results and reply drafts can be inaccurate or incomplete. They are not guaranteed facts or professional advice. The default product direction requires human review before a support reply is sent. Any autonomous action requires separate configuration and risk review.

7. Pricing and usage metering

Private-pilot fees are agreed before activation. Production pricing may combine a base platform fee with variable charges for AI processing or other metered provider consumption. Token and workload calculators are planning tools only; real usage depends on model selection, message history, retrieval context, language and output length.

8. Privacy and support-message data

Depending on configuration, Synvio may process account information, organization membership, inbox addresses, customer message content, message metadata, ticket status, audit events and technical logs. For production use, controller/processor roles, lawful basis, retention, deletion and data-subject procedures should be documented where required.

9. Infrastructure and subprocessors

The current platform can rely on third-party services for hosting, database/authentication, email transport, public forms, website chat and AI processing. Current providers may include Vercel, Supabase, Resend, Formspree and Voiceflow, with an AI model provider added where AI functionality is enabled. Provider selection and regions may evolve.

10. Security

Synvio uses server-side provider secrets and organization-scoped application data in its current architecture. This website does not claim a security certification, completed penetration test or compliance status unless formally documented. Customers should promptly report suspected account compromise or mailbox misconfiguration.

11. Retention, deletion and exports

Pilot retention rules can depend on connected providers and agreed scope. Production customers should have documented retention, backup, deletion and export requirements. Legal or technical retention constraints may apply to some deletion requests.

12. Acceptable use

The service may not be used for unlawful activity, deceptive impersonation, unauthorized mailbox access, spam, prohibited surveillance, malicious messaging or attempts to bypass provider restrictions. Access may be suspended where use creates a material abuse, legal or security risk.

13. Availability and dependencies

The service depends on network availability, third-party providers and customer-controlled systems such as DNS and mailboxes. Provider incidents, API changes, rate limits or configuration changes can affect service. Any guaranteed uptime or service credits must be expressly stated in a signed agreement.

14. Agreement priority and changes

Synvio may update this page as the product evolves. If this website conflicts with a signed order form, service agreement or data-processing agreement, the signed document controls to the extent of the conflict.

15. Contact

Product, privacy and pilot questions can be sent to support@synviocore.xyz.
