Request deletion of your data

Instructions for SYNVIO accounts, support conversations and Instagram connections.

Send a request

Email support@synviocore.xyz with the subject “Data deletion request”. Identify the service or organization and the account or ticket concerned, plus enough context to locate it. Do not send passwords, access tokens or identity-document copies unless a secure, necessary verification method has been agreed.

SYNVIO is operated by Szymon Gałązka. We will confirm the scope and verify identity or authority proportionately before deleting data. A ticket number on its own is insufficient proof.

If you messaged a business

The business you contacted normally controls that conversation. Contact it directly, or ask us to help identify the appropriate controller. We act on that organization’s instructions where SYNVIO is its processor.

For organization administrators

Open Workspace → Company & team → Data deletion. Search by keyword, select conversations or choose all organization conversations, and review the preview before typing DELETE. New conversation activity invalidates the preview. Resume attachment cleanup if the operation reports pending storage removal.

These tools erase active conversation content, messages, associated AI response caches and attachment objects. They preserve minimal deletion and delivery tombstones and necessary accounting records. They do not delete your entire organization, login account, knowledge or configuration.

Instagram and external copies

Disconnect Instagram in FLOW to stop use of that connection. Disconnecting does not automatically erase stored conversation history. You can also revoke the SYNVIO application in your Instagram connected-app settings. Copies held by Meta, email recipients, other processors or backups must be addressed separately under their applicable procedures.

What happens next

We normally respond within one month. Some records may be retained when required by law or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims. We will explain applicable limits and any permitted extension. See the privacy notice for your rights and complaint options.
