Privacy notice

How personal data is used across the SYNVIO website, FLOW and Workspace.

Who is responsible

SYNVIO is operated by Szymon Gałązka. For service accounts, billing, security and direct support, Szymon Gałązka is the data controller. Contact: support@synviocore.xyz.

When a business uses SYNVIO to handle its customer conversations, that business normally determines the purposes and is the controller of those conversations. SYNVIO processes them on its instructions. Contact the business you messaged for decisions about its use of your data; we can help route requests.

Data and purposes

We process account identifiers and contact details to provide access; messages, attachments and company knowledge to provide configured support; Instagram identifiers, permitted profile information and encrypted connection credentials to route messages; and usage, billing and limited technical records to charge correctly, prevent abuse and diagnose failures.

Providing account information needed to sign in is necessary to use the service. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information, passwords or authentication codes.

Legal bases

For our own controller activities, processing necessary to provide the service or answer your request is based on GDPR Article 6(1)(b). Required accounting and other legal records use Article 6(1)(c). Security, fraud prevention and essential service diagnostics use Article 6(1)(f), with our interest in protecting the service and users balanced against individual rights. Where optional processing requires consent, Article 6(1)(a) applies and that consent can be withdrawn. Customer organizations must identify the legal basis for their own conversations.

AI and conversation access

When enabled by an organization, the selected AI provider receives the current conversation context and relevant knowledge to draft or generate a reply. Native AI and a provider chosen through BYOK have separate provider terms. A ticket number alone does not authorize retrieval of earlier conversations. Staff must verify identity and authority before using historical information or changing personal details.

AI can make mistakes. It is not intended to make decisions producing legal or similarly significant effects without human involvement. Contact the organization for human review.

Recipients and international transfers

Configured service providers include Vercel for hosting, Supabase for database, authentication and storage, Postmark for email, Meta for Instagram, and the selected AI provider, including OpenAI when native AI is used. Authorized staff and the business handling a conversation can access data within their permissions. Providers receive only data needed for their service; connected services also apply their own terms.

Provider processing may involve countries outside the EEA. Applicable safeguards must be established for each configured provider, such as an adequacy decision or standard contractual clauses with any required supplementary measures. Contact us for information about the providers, processing locations and safeguards relevant to your use.

Storage and retention

Resolving a ticket or closing it after inactivity does not erase its history. Conversations and attachments remain until deleted under the organization’s retention instructions or an applicable erasure request. Account and operational information is kept while needed to provide and secure the service; records required by law are retained for the applicable obligation. We do not promise a universal deletion period across all customers and external providers.

Deletion tools remove selected content from the active application database and attachment storage. Backups, provider records, necessary financial records and legally required evidence may have separate retention periods and must be handled through the corresponding procedures.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability and object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. We may request proportionate identity or authority verification. Contact support@synviocore.xyz; requests concerning a customer’s conversations may need to be handled by that controller.

We normally respond within one month, subject to the GDPR rules for extensions. You can complain to your supervisory authority, including Poland’s President of the Personal Data Protection Office (UODO).

Browser storage and updates

Authentication, preferences and the chat interface use necessary browser storage. Starting the AI chat loads the configured chat service; submitted messages are processed to answer you. This release does not add advertising analytics. We will update this notice when processing materially changes.
