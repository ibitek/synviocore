Data protection at SYNVIO

Practical controls for organizations using AI and human support together.

People remain accountable

Each customer organization controls the purposes, access and retention of its customer conversations. SYNVIO provides technical controls; a software feature or system prompt alone cannot certify GDPR compliance. A processing agreement and accurate provider and retention records are still required where applicable.

Access and AI boundaries

Organization and channel access checks apply on the server. The AI handled queue lets authorized employees review current conversations and take ownership. Taking ownership stops subsequent automatic AI replies. The assistant receives current-ticket context; guessing a ticket number does not grant access to older records.

Knowledge and employee procedures

Workspace knowledge can be organized into general information, legal information, privacy and operational procedures. Provide approved instructions on identity verification, escalation and restricted disclosures. AI guidance is advisory. Employees remain responsible for verifying a requester before sharing historical personal data or changing sensitive account details.

Deletion and retention

Administrators can find conversations, preview a deletion scope and erase selected or all organization conversations. This does not delete the organization account, configuration, company knowledge, necessary billing totals or every external-provider record. See deletion instructions or contact support@synviocore.xyz for broader requests.

Security and transparency

Instagram connection tokens are encrypted at rest; application logs use status metadata instead of message text or credentials. Authorized application components still process readable messages to deliver support. SYNVIO is not an end-to-end encrypted messaging service. For controller details, legal bases, recipients and your rights, read our privacy notice.
