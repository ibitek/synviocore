Platform architecture

One backend. Separate companies. Multiple channels.

01 / Multi-tenant

Each company gets its own workspace.

Organizations, memberships, inboxes, tickets and messages are scoped per tenant. You do not deploy a new Vercel or Supabase for every client.

02 / Channel adapters

Email now. Web chat next.

The conversation model stays common while adapters handle Resend/custom email, later Gmail/Microsoft, web chat and additional channels.

03 / Human control

AI assists; the team owns the final answer.

Drafting and triage can reduce repetitive work without silently turning every customer message into an autonomous send.

Commercial model
