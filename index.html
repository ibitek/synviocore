Pricing

Pay for the work that actually happened.

Every AI action is billed from what that action used — typically about 0.5¢ to 2¢. Every message your team sends or receives is 0.4¢. A conversation with no AI and no messages adds $0.

Start with $2
Every workspace opens with $2 in the wallet.

That credit is enough to try the panel, Copilot, knowledge setup and the API thoroughly. Configuration is meant to stay simple: connect a channel, invite people, spend only when you use the product.

Automatic plan proposal
$25 per seat when PAYG would exceed $25 × the team.

Seats include every agent and every admin. When pay-as-you-go costs pass $25 × that headcount, Synvio proposes a $25/seat monthly plan. The plan unlocks a month of usage equal to $25 × seats × 10 of pay-as-you-go value. You are not enrolled automatically — the product only recommends the switch.

Why this is hard to lose money on

You are not paying a platform tax for idle seats on PAYG. Unused AI is $0. The wallet is visible on the admin page before spend happens. If credit runs out, AI pauses instead of running an open invoice. The $25 plan is offered only after PAYG would already have crossed $25 per person — and then it covers ten times that amount for the month, so high volume becomes a ceiling, not a surprise.

Data & independence

We do not sit in your customer database.

Synvio does not receive access to your CRM, customer databases or employee passwords. AI is not trained on your support conversations. Synvio does not keep a separate warehouse of your source data — the service runs on independent platforms that you can audit on their own terms.

Supabase

Workspace accounts and conversation records on an independent database platform.

Postmark

Transactional email delivery, separate from Synvio’s application servers.

OpenAI

Generation only. Your utterances are not used to train models for Synvio.

TikTok

Official TikTok business APIs for chats — not unofficial scraping or personal logins.
