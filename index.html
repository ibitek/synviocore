One operating view

Your support team works from one queue.

Email conversations and AI chat handoffs arrive in the same workspace. Agents see the customer history, write the reply and keep ownership without switching tools.

Email

Use the company’s own support address.

Customer email is routed into the correct company workspace and answered from the configured company identity.

AI chat

Let AI handle routine website questions.

The website assistant can resolve repetitive questions using approved company knowledge instead of pushing every visitor into an agent queue.

Human handoff

A customer can ask for a person without starting over.

The conversation moves into the same Synvio inbox with the previous chat context attached, so an employee can continue from where the AI stopped.

AI chat → human

Automation ends exactly where your team should begin.

The AI web-chat layer is being added around escalation, not around hiding the human team. The rollout is designed so a visitor can request an employee, or the assistant can hand off when the conversation needs judgment.
