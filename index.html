A practical example

1,000 incoming emails + 1,000 outgoing emails = $5 in message fees. If AI also uses 1 million uncached input tokens and 200,000 output tokens, add $3.60. Total usage: $8.60, before any handoffs or other usage.

Before you start

A few practical answers.

Do I need both Flow and Workspace?

No. Flow can answer website or Instagram messages with AI. Workspace can support a human team without AI. Connect them when you need handoff.

Does Copilot send replies automatically?

No. Copilot prepares a private, editable draft. Use draft moves it into the reply editor; an employee still sends it.

Can I use my own AI provider?

Yes. Organization-level BYOK uses your configured provider. Synvio AI charges are zero with BYOK; message and handoff fees still apply.

Can Shopify tools issue refunds or change an order?

No. The current Commerce tools read approved product, inventory, order and fulfilment information. They do not issue refunds or modify orders.

How do I fund the wallet?

Contact the team to arrange funding. The calculator estimates usage; it does not take a payment. Self-service checkout is not available yet.
