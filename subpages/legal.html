Legal & privacy

Terms for using Synvio.

These terms cover the website and use of the Synvio customer-support platform. A signed customer agreement can add or replace terms for a specific deployment.

1. Synvio service

Synvio provides software for customer-support operations, including email handling, support workflows, AI-assisted features and web-chat handoff. Feature availability can depend on the customer’s selected configuration and connected channels.

2. Customer accounts and team access

Customers are responsible for protecting login credentials and inviting only authorized employees. Administrators control company configuration and team invitations. Agent accounts are intended for customer-support work and do not receive administrator configuration rights.

3. Mailboxes and company domains

A customer may connect only mailboxes, sender identities and domains it is authorized to use. Custom-domain onboarding may require DNS changes approved by the customer’s domain administrator.

4. AI-assisted output

AI-generated classifications, summaries and drafts can be inaccurate. Customers should review outputs before relying on them. Synvio does not provide legal, medical, financial or other regulated professional advice through AI-generated content.

5. AI chat and human handoff

Where web chat is enabled, automated responses may be used for routine support. Human handoff is designed to transfer the conversation context to an authorized support employee when escalation is requested or configured.

6. Pricing and usage

Email: $0.002 incoming / $0.003 outgoing. Website chat: incoming messages are free; each bot or employee reply costs $0.002. Human handoff adds $0.002 once per conversation. AI usage is charged separately unless BYOK is enabled. The 30-day plan costs $25 per support agent and includes $60 of pooled Synvio usage per agent, covering channel fees and SYNVIO/CORE AI. Administrators are included; there is a minimum of one paid seat. Purchase is separate from PAYG deposits and earns no deposit bonus. No automatic renewal. After expiry or exhaustion, paid operations pause unless an administrator opts into PAYG. Existing plans retain their purchased allowance. Each deposit earns a smoothly increasing wallet bonus: 0% at $2, rising to 60% at $25. Above $25 the rate stays at 60%; the dollar bonus still grows. The formula is 60% × (3t² − 2t³), where t = min(1, max(0, (deposit − 2) / 23)). Deposits are calculated separately, never combined retroactively. A $25 deposit gives $25 paid credit plus $15 Wallet bonus. Separate small deposits do not qualify for a larger cumulative bonus. Bonus credits cover platform AI, email, chat and handoff. They cannot pay external BYOK bills or buy subscriptions and cannot be withdrawn. SYNVIO/CORE AI rates: $4 per million input tokens, $1 cached input and $16 output. Current payments are owner-managed tests; no card checkout is enabled. Usage estimates are not invoices. Paid balances and Wallet bonuses do not expire under the current model; plan allowances expire after 30 days.

7. Customer data and privacy

Synvio may process account information, support messages, customer contact details, conversation metadata, company knowledge, audit data and technical logs when needed to provide the service. The applicable customer agreement should define controller/processor responsibilities, retention and data-subject procedures where required.

8. Service providers and BYOK

Synvio may rely on infrastructure, authentication, email, form, chat and AI service providers to operate selected features. Current infrastructure includes Vercel, Supabase and Postmark. AI processing uses OpenAI by default or OpenAI, DeepSeek or Anthropic under BYOK. Bring your own OpenAI, DeepSeek or Anthropic API key. With BYOK enabled, Synvio charges $0 for AI; you pay the provider directly. Message and handoff fees still apply. Provider errors never silently switch you to paid SYNVIO/CORE AI. Supported features depend on the selected model; DeepSeek and Anthropic adapters currently support text. Saved BYOK credentials are encrypted server-side and shared at organization level. Administrators may remove them in either app. Removing a saved key does not revoke it with its provider. Requests already in progress may finish. Confirmation is required before removal switches future usage to paid SYNVIO/CORE AI.

9. Security

Customers must promptly report suspected unauthorized access or account compromise. No statement on this website should be interpreted as a certification, audit result or guaranteed security standard unless expressly provided in a signed agreement.

10. Acceptable use

Synvio may not be used for unlawful activity, spam, deceptive impersonation, unauthorized access, malware, prohibited surveillance or attempts to bypass service-provider restrictions.

11. Availability and changes

Features may evolve as the product is improved. Service levels, support commitments, warranties and production obligations apply only where they are expressly included in a signed customer agreement.
