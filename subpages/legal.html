Terms of Service & Responsible AI

A practical framework describing how Synvio AI services may be configured, tested and deployed. Final production commitments, service levels and data-processing terms are defined in the applicable written agreement with each client.

1. Scope of Services

Synvio AI designs and configures AI-assisted communication workflows that may include message classification, routing, translation, response drafting, knowledge retrieval, CRM or database actions, and human escalation. Available capabilities depend on the integrations, providers and configuration selected for a particular deployment.

2. AI-Assisted Outputs & Human Oversight

AI-generated classifications, translations and drafts are probabilistic outputs and should not be treated as guaranteed facts. Production workflows can be configured with confidence thresholds, approved knowledge sources, restricted actions, review queues and human approval steps. Clients remain responsible for deciding which actions may be automated and which require review.

3. Demonstrations & Performance Claims

Interactive examples, sample messages and processing visualizations on this website are demonstrations of intended workflow behavior. They are not benchmarks, service-level guarantees or representations that identical results will occur in every environment. Production performance depends on message complexity, connected systems, model providers, rate limits, network conditions and client policies.

4. Data Handling & Third-Party Providers

Data handling is determined by the production architecture and the third-party services selected for a deployment. Where external AI, messaging, hosting, CRM or database providers are used, their respective terms and data-processing practices also apply. Synvio AI will document the intended data flow for a client deployment before production activation.

5. Security & Access Controls

Production integrations should use least-privilege credentials, environment-managed secrets, access restrictions and audit logging where supported. No website statement should be interpreted as a guarantee that a particular encryption standard, retention policy or security certification applies unless it is expressly stated in the client’s written agreement or the relevant provider documentation.

6. Client Configuration & Responsibilities

Clients are responsible for supplying lawful access to connected systems, accurate business rules, approved knowledge sources and instructions concerning escalation, retention and permitted automated actions. Clients must review high-impact workflows before enabling autonomous dispatch or data modification.

7. Acceptable Use

Services may not be used to facilitate unlawful activity, deceptive impersonation, unauthorized access, malicious messaging, prohibited surveillance or other activity that violates applicable law or third-party platform rules. Synvio AI may decline or suspend configurations that create material security, abuse or compliance risks.

8. Availability, Maintenance & Dependencies

Unless a separate service-level agreement states otherwise, availability is provided on a commercially reasonable basis. Maintenance windows, upstream provider incidents, API changes, rate limits and failures in client-controlled systems may affect functionality. Any guaranteed uptime, response time or service credits must be stated in a separate written agreement.

9. Intellectual Property & Client Materials

Clients retain rights to their own data, content, credentials and business materials. Ownership and licensing of custom workflow code, templates, connectors or deployment-specific assets will be defined in the applicable proposal or service agreement.

10. Changes, Testing & Production Activation

New workflows should be validated in a test environment before production use. Material changes to prompts, routing rules, connected data sources or autonomous actions may require re-testing. Synvio AI may update website descriptions and demonstration features as the product evolves.

11. Limitation of Website Information

Information on this website is provided for product evaluation and does not by itself create a service-level commitment, legal guarantee or data-processing agreement. Specific obligations arise from the written commercial agreement accepted by the relevant parties.

12. Contact & Agreement Priority

Questions about a proposed deployment should be raised before production activation. If these website terms conflict with a signed proposal, order form, data-processing agreement or other negotiated contract, the signed agreement controls to the extent of the conflict.
