How Synvio works for support teams, administrators and customer conversations.

Flow builds and publishes your website assistant. Workspace brings email and chat conversations into a shared support inbox. Both use the same organization, wallet and accounts.

Bring your own OpenAI, DeepSeek or Anthropic API key. With BYOK enabled, Synvio charges $0 for AI; you pay the provider directly. Message and handoff fees still apply. Provider errors never silently switch you to paid SYNVIO/CORE AI. Supported features depend on the selected model; DeepSeek and Anthropic adapters currently support text.

An organization administrator can remove the saved key from either Flow or Workspace, including at zero balance. The setting is shared across both apps. Removal asks for confirmation because future requests use paid SYNVIO/CORE AI. It does not revoke the key at the provider or cancel an in-flight request.

SYNVIO/CORE AI is used by default, billed from actual tokens at the published rates. Channel fees are separate.

Email: $0.002 incoming / $0.003 outgoing. Website chat: incoming messages are free; each bot or employee reply costs $0.002. Human handoff adds $0.002 once per conversation. AI usage is charged separately unless BYOK is enabled.

Each deposit earns a smoothly increasing wallet bonus: 0% at $2, rising to 60% at $25. Above $25 the rate stays at 60%; the dollar bonus still grows. The formula is 60% × (3t² − 2t³), where t = min(1, max(0, (deposit − 2) / 23)). Deposits are calculated separately, never combined retroactively. A $25 deposit gives $25 paid credit plus $15 Wallet bonus. Separate small deposits do not qualify for a larger cumulative bonus. Bonus credits cover platform AI, email, chat and handoff. They cannot pay external BYOK bills or buy subscriptions and cannot be withdrawn.

The 30-day plan costs $25 per support agent and includes $60 of pooled Synvio usage per agent, covering channel fees and SYNVIO/CORE AI. Administrators are included; there is a minimum of one paid seat. Purchase is separate from PAYG deposits and earns no deposit bonus. No automatic renewal. After expiry or exhaustion, paid operations pause unless an administrator opts into PAYG. Existing plans retain their purchased allowance.
